Why this is an overlay, not a forecast feature

Financial stress has almost no correlation with next quarter's ag revenue (|corr|≈0.1) — spikes are sudden and mean-revert, so a quarterly model learns nothing from it. But it reacts to exogenous shocks weeks before they reach quarterly data. So it can't predict the pandemic; it detects the shock ~1 quarter early and widens forecast intervals / dampens demand while the shock is live.
